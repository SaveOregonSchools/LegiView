What this run does

It scans the complete official session catalogue, then collects supported legislative measures only for sessions at or after the verified 2014R1 boundary. It syncs source entities page by page, reconciles required OLIS displays, normalizes durable document records, and records explicit completeness. It does not start Download Archive.
